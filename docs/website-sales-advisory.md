# Website Sales advisory and buyer decisions

This additive candidate follows migrations202–209. Migration210 retains the
canonical CRM boundary and introduces immutable advisory records, scoped private
jobs, buyer grants and byte-verified protected video assets. Existing grants gain
no advisory permission automatically. No Production migration or activation is
performed by adding these files.

The workbench area “Beratung und Entscheidung” connects nine capabilities:

1. Customer value scenarios with explicit assumptions, NET costs, exact minor
   units and amortization. Saved capacity is separate from assumed cash savings.
2. Adaptive SPIN questions based on the exact current customer brief and confirmed
   answers. A suggestion never invents an answer.
3. An approved buyer decision packet with options, limitations and shared steps.
   A named authenticated account can confirm a step or ask a question; this is
   not an offer acceptance, contract signature or payment authorization.
4. Website/demo advice selected from exact approved excerpts. Buyer questions
   enter a moderated internal preparation/review/publication chain.
5. Permission-bound transcripts and exact quoted analysis candidates. Separate
   human review records decisions without silently changing canonical facts.
6. Rights-bearing knowledge, observable coaching criteria and continuing internal
   AI roleplay. Simulated customer turns are explicitly labelled simulations.
7. Prospective experiment plans, private unpredictable assignment seeds,
   immutable company assignments, original denominators and stopping decisions.
   Pending, unknown and incomplete outcomes are distinct from negative results.
8. Explainable forecasts based on actual recorded treatment origins and results.
   Retrospective time holdout is labelled post-hoc and requires review. Insufficient
   evidence produces no probability. A prediction never supplies its own labels.
9. Actual inert demo recording in a sealed local browser, approved visible
   captions, measured bounded WebM output and a protected buyer player with a
   text alternative. This implementation does not synthesize a voice or avatar.

Every derived record binds exact current sources. Expiry, correction, revoked
rights, changed parent versions or incomplete bounded history block reuse.
Project-level human authority and narrow machine resource unions are evaluated
separately. A read response cannot enlarge a machine's resource grants.

## Buyer access and recovery

The copyable link stores a random32-byte grant only in its fragment. Requests use
the same-origin `x-sales-review-token` header; URLs, normal logs and command
journals receive no raw grant. Internal creation receives its SHA256 hash.
Reads are explicitly whitelisted and omit internal margins, contact notes,
training sources and hourly costs. A ROI projection needs the current exact
approved commercial offer in addition to the packet approval.

Writes require native password/MFA account context, current account-to-grant
binding and CSRF. The original actor binding, packet, grant/step versions,
idempotency key, correlation and exact body survive an ambiguous outcome.
Recovery never authorizes a different account or a newly generated command.
Revocation, expiry or source changes deny later page and asset requests. A
downloaded copy cannot be remotely erased; the open player clears its temporary
object URL when its fresh read loses access.

## Private execution

The model worker accepts POST at `/api/cron/website-sales-advisory` with the
host-only worker secret. It chooses only fixed configured profiles, never a
request-body tenant or workload. Global pause applies. Disabled AI configuration
leaves queued jobs READY. A claimed job uses an opaque executor, fresh saved
grant/member checks, a lease, normal durable AI credit reservation/settlement and
the existing approved model policy. There are no model tools or external-send
capabilities. Unknown provider/settlement/completion outcomes are held UNKNOWN
and are not automatically retried. Exact citations and review requirements are
validated again before the result is persisted.

Each fixed profile carries the actual saved workload/grant `authorityReference`.
Configuration/revision freshness is checked separately; changing a deployment
does not invent a different business-authority reference.

VIDEO runs in a dedicated checked-out Node/browser process, outside the Vercel
route bundle. Its actual clean repository revision/origin and private database
target are verified; it does not impersonate a Vercel deployment. The worker
loads the current immutable demo and independent QA certificate through the
private store, renders only its verified HTML/assets without outside network,
checks visible captions, normalizes recording-clock overhead with the matching
Playwright FFmpeg, measures final duration and stores actual bytes atomically.
The buyer endpoint rechecks access and byte hash before delivery.

Four optional settings are server-only, production-only metadata:
`CRM_WEBSITE_SALES_ADVISORY_HOST`,
`CRM_WEBSITE_SALES_ADVISORY_EXECUTOR_DATABASE_URL`,
`CRM_WEBSITE_SALES_ADVISORY_WORKER_SECRET`, and
`CRM_WEBSITE_SALES_ADVISORY_AI`. Their classification neither provisions secrets
nor proves remote-value parity, model funding, production gates or activation.
The private database login must not be the ordinary tenant runtime or owner.

The final evidence receipt distinguishes local synthetic business data, actual
browser/database/SDK execution and any later provider-owned release checks.
Teams, A1, customer sends, signatures, invoices, payments, paid subscriptions and
recurring activation are outside this change.
