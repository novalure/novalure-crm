Estate agency proposal form: choosing a design is optional; Novalure can help choose. To send the enquiry, provide the agency name, contact name, a valid email address and consent; phone and website/domain are optional. Optional details include branding, desired pages, property-search filters, listing display and current property CRM/feed; availability and scope are confirmed in the proposal.

The optional valuation-enquiry quiz has a separately quoted price. Its public demo requires choices for intent, property type, number of bedrooms and timing, as well as a town or county, contact name, valid email and consent. Phone number, street address and Eircode are optional. A test submission goes to Novalure; it does not provide a valuation or book an appointment. Use test information for the demo.
