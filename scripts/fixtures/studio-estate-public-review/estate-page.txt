GOOD TO KNOW Questions? Clear answers. Everything you need to know before you choose. If you are unsure about anything, leave it for our conversation. How does it work? Choose a design or ask us to help you decide, share a few details about your agency and tell us whether you would like the optional valuation quiz. We will discuss your needs and prepare a tailored proposal before any work begins. What do I need to provide? To get started, your agency name and a way to contact you are enough. If you have a logo, photos, text, website address or PSRA details, you can share them later. Do I need to know anything technical? No. Tell us a little about your agency and choose a design if you already have a favourite. We will discuss the technical details with you in plain English. Can you help with text and images? Yes. Let us know what you already have and where you would like help. We will agree any additional content or image work in your proposal. Can the design match my agency? Yes. We can adapt the selected direction with your branding, images and content. We will agree the scope and any individual work in your proposal. Can my properties appear on the website? We can discuss how to present your listings and whether your existing software provides a suitable authorised feed. Integration depends on your provider and will be confirmed before it is included. Can I add the property valuation funnel? Yes. The interactive valuation enquiry funnel is an optional add-on. Try it in any design preview, then select it in your brief. The preview is illustrative. If you choose to submit it, the test request is sent to Novalure; it does not book a visit or provide a valuation. Can I keep my existing domain? Usually you can keep a domain you already own. We will check how it is managed and agree any connection or transfer steps with you. What happens after my site is launched? Hosting, updates, support and any future changes depend on the care arrangement we agree with you. We will explain what is included before you decide. How much does it cost and how long does it take?
